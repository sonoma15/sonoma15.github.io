How to Play Universal Grid

1. Fill the 3×3 Grid: Select a cell and name a Universal Parks ride or attraction from any of the 9 Universal parks worldwide that satisfies both criteria.

2. No Location Spoilers: Park locations are hidden while searching—multi-park attractions count for any park where they operate.

3. 9 Guesses: In standard daily play, you have 9 guesses total. Switch between Standard and Park Nerd difficulty anytime without losing progress!
