How to Play Universal Grid

1. Fill the 3×3 Grid: Select a cell and name a Universal Parks ride, show, or attraction from any of the 9 Universal parks worldwide that satisfies both the Row and Column criteria.

2. No Location Spoilers: Park locations are hidden while searching—you have to know which parks each ride operates in! Multi-park attractions count for any park where they operate.

3. 9 Guesses: In standard play, you have 9 guesses total—wrong guesses still consume a guess! Toggle ∞ Guesses or switch to Unlimited Practice anytime.

4. Rarity Score: Every valid answer carries a pick percentage. Picking deep-cut attractions lowers your Rarity Score. Unsolved cells add 100 points.
